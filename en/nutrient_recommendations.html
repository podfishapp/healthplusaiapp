Nutrition Recommendations – Information and References

This page provides detailed information about the nutrition recommendations calculated by our app. Our calculations are based on established methods and guidelines from reputable sources in nutrition science.

Calculation Method

The basal metabolic rate (BMR) is estimated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation:

  • For men: BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age + 5
  • For women: BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age − 161

An activity multiplier is then applied based on the user's activity level. Finally, the calorie goal is adjusted according to the user's primary goal (weight loss, maintenance, or weight gain).

Macro Distribution

The app also provides macro recommendations based on the following distribution of total calories:

  • Protein: 30% (4 kcal per gram)
  • Fat: 25% (9 kcal per gram)
  • Carbohydrates: 45% (4 kcal per gram)

This distribution is in line with commonly accepted nutritional guidelines. In particular, the percentage breakdown aligns with recommendations from authoritative bodies such as the British Nutrition Foundation and the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics, which provide further context and validation for these macro nutrient targets.

References and Sources

  1. Mifflin-St Jeor Equation Study – A study validating the accuracy of the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for estimating basal metabolic rate.
  2. Dietary Guidelines for Americans, 2020–2025 (PDF) – U.S. government guidelines recommend that adults obtain 45–65% of their daily calories from carbohydrates, 10–35% from protein, and 20–35% from fat, which form the basis of our app's macronutrient distribution.
